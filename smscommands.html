SMS Commands

Manage your Power24 account via SMS from anywhere, anytime.

Register via SMS

SMS "your METERNO" to 32904 to Register
e.g. "12345678901"

Account Management

SMS "LAST ELEC" to 32904 to view your previously purchased tokens
e.g. "LAST ELEC"
SMS "FREE" to 32904 to receive your FBE token
e.g. "FREE"
SMS "HELP ELEC" to 32904 to get help details
e.g. "HELP ELEC"

Multiple Meter Management

SMS "ADD METERNO" to 32904 to Register another Meter No to your profile
e.g. "ADD 12345678901"
SMS "DELETE METERNO" to 32904 to remove a Meter No from your profile
e.g. "DELETE 12345678901"
SMS "DEFAULT METERNO" to 32904 to set a Meter No as your default
e.g. "DEFAULT 12345678901"
SMS "LAST METERNO" to 32904 to get the last tokens for a specific Meter No
e.g. "LAST 12345678901"
SMS "LIST" to 32904 to get a list of all the Meter No on your profile
e.g. "LIST"

All SMS messages are charged at standard VAS rates.
