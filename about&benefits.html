About Power24

Learn how to buy prepaid electricity easily and conveniently.

How It Works

Don't run out of electricity ever again! Register with your meter number and cellphone number on Power24.


Purchase prepaid electricity easily and conveniently within the comfort of your own home or your office, via internet or cell phone banking and receive your electricity token almost instantly via SMS.

Benefits

  • Easy registration
  • No logins needed
  • No credit card required
  • No cash wallets or withdrawals
  • No service fees
  • Tokens received via SMS

How to Buy Prepaid Electricity

1

Register your cellphone number and prepaid meter

Register via SMS or on the Power24 Sign Up page. Registration requires your cell number so we may SMS your token to you when purchasing electricity and will also be used as your payment reference.

2

Create a beneficiary on your bank account

Create a new beneficiary on your bank account for our bank account (use the same bank). We have an account at each of the major banks to ensure that funds get cleared almost instantly. e.g. If you bank with ABSA, setup a beneficiary with our ABSA account details.

3

Transfer funds and receive your token

Transfer funds from your bank account to the newly created beneficiary using the cellphone number you registered with as the reference. We will SMS your prepaid electricity token directly to your phone. This process takes place within 5 minutes.

Note: Power24 relies on other 3rd parties like banks, municipalities and other service providers. Power24 cannot guarantee this process time.
