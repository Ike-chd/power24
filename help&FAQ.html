Help & FAQ's

Frequently asked questions about Power24 prepaid electricity.

How do I know if I can buy Prepaid Electricity for my area?

Check that your meter is supported by power24.co.za by going to Check Meter or take a look if your local municipality is registered with us here.

How can I get my previously purchased Tokens?

SMS "LAST ELEC" to 32904 to receive an SMS with your previous electricity tokens, or look up your previously purchased electricity tokens now by going to the Check Last Token page. SMS's are charged at standard VAS rates.

What is the minimum amount I can buy Prepaid Electricity for?

The minimum purchase amount may differ between the major banks:

  • ABSA bank: R100.00
  • FNB bank: R110.00
  • Nedbank: R100.00
  • Standard Bank: R100.00

What is the maximum amount I can buy Prepaid Electricity for?

Most of the municipalities and service providers have a maximum purchase amount. We suggest buying for a maximum of R1000 at a time. If you need e.g. R2000 then do 2 payments for R1000.

If you happen to make a payment above the municipal maximum you will receive multiple tokens via SMS to add up to the value of your payment. Make sure to check all received tokens, or check your last purchases here.

What if I have multiple prepaid electricity meters?

Buying your pre-paid electricity is still quick and easy for all your meters. Simply register your primary meter either via the Sign Up page or SMS. Then add your other meters to your profile by SMSing "ADD meterno" to 32904, e.g. "ADD 0123456789" for each of your meters.

When making a purchase, use the cellphone number you registered with as a reference when purchasing for your primary meter. When purchasing for your other meter/s, use the meterno as your reference.

Are there any hidden costs?

No, you'll get the exact amount of electricity units for the amount that you transferred. Only the SMS's are charged at standard VAS rates. The amount of electricity units received is calculated in accordance with your current municipal tariff rates.

  • NO Login required.
  • NO Credit card details submitted or required.
  • NO Cash "wallets" or withdrawals.
  • NO Service fees.

How do I receive my token after making a payment?

Once a payment reflects in our bank account, your token will be generated and sent directly to you via SMS. This process relies on the municipality creating your token and can take up to 10 minutes.

How long should it take for me to receive my token after making a payment?

Once a payment reflects in our bank account your token will be generated. This process relies on the rules and time frames defined by the individual banks.

  • Standard Bank to Standard Bank payments may take up to 24hrs to reflect.
  • Standard Bank to another bank may take up to 3 business days.
  • Payments made from a Mastercard account may take up to 24hrs to reflect.

What are the added benefits of using power24.co.za?

  • Purchase anytime of the day or night.
  • Receive your token within minutes.

Reasons for not receiving Token from power24.co.za?

  • Your account might be in arrears.
  • System might respond with consumer / meter blocked.
  • Municipality might be down at the time token is being requested.
  • There could be a delay from the bank with notification of payment.
  • The reference used might be incorrect or no reference provided.

Prepaid Meter Installation?

Unfortunately Power24.co.za does not install prepaid electricity meters. Should you require a prepaid meter installed, please contact your municipality. Meter installations may only be done through your municipality and by a qualified technician from the municipality. Please contact the prepaid department at your municipality for further assistance.

All SMS messages are charged at standard VAS rates.
