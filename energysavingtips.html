Energy Saving Tips

Easy ways to save energy without costing you.

Reduce Your Energy Consumption

  • Air dry dishes instead of using your dishwasher's drying cycle.
  • Turn things off when you are not in the room such as lights, TVs, entertainment systems, and your computer and monitor.
  • Turn the plugs off when the equipment is not in use — TVs and DVDs in standby mode still use several watts of power.
  • Reduce the temperature on your geyser to 55 degrees Celsius.
  • Take short showers instead of baths and use low-flow shower heads for additional energy savings.
  • Wash only full loads of dishes and clothes instead of numerous smaller loads.
  • Air dry clothes instead of using the tumble dryer.
  • Check to see that windows and doors are closed when heating or cooling your home.
  • Boil only the water you need instead of boiling a full pot or kettle every time.
